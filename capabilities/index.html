Research & Data Analysis

The right answer begins with the right evidence.

The decisions that carry the most weight are made with the least reliable information. Berecons LLC changes that. We design research programmes, manage data collection, conduct rigorous analysis, qualitative and quantitative, and translate findings into intelligence that decision-makers can actually use. We do not produce reports. We produce clarity.

  • Research Design & Methodology The framework before the fieldwork. Right questions, right methods, right controls.
  • Qualitative Research Interviews, focus groups, ethnographic approaches, document analysis. We go beyond data to understand reality.
  • Quantitative Analysis Statistical modelling, survey design, data visualisation, impact metrics. Numbers made meaningful.
  • Outcome Monitoring & Impact Evaluation We measure what matters, not what is easy to count.
  • Academic & Professional Research Support Expert partnership for researchers, students, and institutions where methodology is non-negotiable.

Content Development

Language in service of strategy.

Communication is not the final step in a consulting process; it is a discipline in itself. We develop content that does exactly what it needs to do: inform, persuade, report, position, or explain.

Our work is human-authored with precision. Not because AI cannot produce words, but because the right words, in the right register, for the right audience, require judgment that comes from understanding your context, not just your brief.

Precision language is not a luxury. It is the difference between a recommendation that lands and one that does not.

Systems + Design Thinking

Understand the system before you change it.

Most organisations do not have a strategy problem. They have a systems problem; a failure to see how their parts connect, where the friction lives, and what change would actually change anything.

We apply systems thinking and design thinking as complementary lenses: mapping complexity, identifying root causes, and designing interventions that address the system, not the symptom. This is diagnostic and design work at the institutional level.

  • Systems Mapping & Analysis Diagramming and analysing complex systems: organisational, programmatic, technological, or market-level.
  • Design Thinking Facilitation Structured problem framing, ideation, and solution prototyping. Workshop-based and consultant-led.
  • Process Review & Redesign Identifying inefficiencies, redundancies, and failure points, then designing better processes.
  • Innovation Scoping For organisations exploring new products, services, or operating models. Structured divergence before disciplined convergence.
  • Institutional Diagnostics Deep-dive assessment of organisational health, capability gaps, and structural misalignments.

Strategy Consulting

Direction that holds under scrutiny.

Strategy is not a plan. It is a set of choices: where to compete, what to build, what to stop doing, and what to protect.

Our job is to help you make those choices with the clarity that comes from evidence, and the confidence that comes from rigour. We provide independent strategic advisory to organisations navigating growth, transformation, competition, or change.

We do not tell clients what they want to hear. We tell them what the evidence says.

Our recommendations are built to survive the pressures of implementation, not just presentation.

  • Business Strategy & Development Market analysis, competitive positioning, growth strategy. Grounded in evidence, pressure-tested against context.
  • Organisational Strategy Structure, governance, capability alignment. What your organisation needs to become to do what you need it to do.
  • Business Planning Plans that are realistic, fundable, and executable. Not produced for funders and filed.
  • Stakeholder & Partnership Strategy Who to work with, on what terms, and toward what end.
  • Market Entry & Expansion For organisations entering new markets or scaling operations, particularly within the West African and broader African context.

Groundwork

Your Investment. Your Property. Your Protection.

Groundwork is Berecons LLC’s dedicated protection service for clients making property, investment, relocation, and local execution decisions in Ghana.

It is designed for clients who need trusted local support before money moves, documents are signed, assets are acquired, or projects begin. The service brings together verification, due diligence, advisory, representation, registration support, dispute support, relocation support, and asset oversight.

Groundwork exists to help clients move with confidence in Ghana by making risk visible, documented, and manageable before decisions become difficult to reverse.
  • Land & Property Acquisition Security Independent title checks, ownership verification, site plan review, boundary confirmation, double-allocation screening, and acquisition risk checks.
  • Due Diligence & Forensic Research Background checks on companies, vendors, partners, contractors, investment opportunities, and project claims before capital is committed.
  • Legal Advisory & Compliance Support Contract review, statutory process guidance, registration advisory, permit support, and regulatory navigation.
  • Local Representation & Project Oversight On-the-ground support for remote investors and busy clients, including site visits, meeting attendance, progress reporting, milestone checks, and payment verification.
  • Asset & Property Registration Support Support with indentures, site plans, stamping, Lands Commission processes, title registration, inheritance registration, and documentation follow-through.
  • Relocation & Concierge Settlement Support for returnees, expats, families, and companies settling in Ghana, including property search, neighbourhood guidance, school and service mapping, and local setup support.
  • Dispute Resolution & Asset Recovery Support Documentation review, breach analysis, mediation support, contractor default documentation, fund tracing, and escalation advisory.
  • Portfolio Asset Management & Investment Structuring Portfolio audits, documentation review, tenancy advisory, maintenance oversight, rental yield benchmarking, succession planning, and exit strategy support.
