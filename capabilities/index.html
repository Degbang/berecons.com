01

Research & Data Analysis

The right answer begins with the right evidence.

The decisions that carry the most weight are made with the least reliable information. Berecons changes that by designing research programmes, managing data collection, conducting rigorous analysis, and translating findings into intelligence that decision-makers can actually use. We do not produce reports. We produce clarity.

  • Research Design & Methodology
  • Qualitative Research
  • Quantitative Analysis
  • Outcome Monitoring & Impact Evaluation
  • Academic & Professional Research Support

Content Development

Language in service of strategy.

Communication is not the final step in a consulting process; it is a discipline in itself. We develop content that does exactly what it needs to do: inform, persuade, report, position, or explain. Our work is human-authored with precision.

Precision language is not a luxury. It is the difference between a recommendation that lands and one that does not.

03

Systems + Design Analysis

Understand the system before you change it.

Most organisations do not have a strategy problem. They have a systems problem: a failure to see how their parts connect, where the friction lives, and what change would actually change anything. We apply systems thinking and design thinking as complementary lenses, mapping complexity, identifying root causes, and designing interventions that address the system, not the symptom.

  • Systems Mapping & Analysis
  • Design Thinking Facilitation
  • Process Review & Redesign
  • Innovation Scoping
  • Institutional Diagnostics

Strategy Consulting

Direction that holds under scrutiny.

Strategy is not a plan. It is a set of choices: where to compete, what to build, what to stop doing, and what to protect. We provide independent strategic advisory to organisations navigating growth, transformation, competition, or change.

We do not tell clients what they want to hear. We tell them what the evidence says.
  • Business Strategy & Development
  • Organisational Strategy
  • Business Planning

06

Groundwork

Your Investment. Your Property. Your Protection.

Groundwork is Berecons LLC’s dedicated protection service for clients making property, investment, relocation, and local execution decisions in Ghana. It is designed for those who need trusted local support before money moves, documents are signed, or projects begin.

Groundwork combines verification, legal coordination, local representation, and documented oversight before decisions become difficult to reverse.

  • Due Diligence & Forensic Research
  • Legal Advisory & Compliance Support
  • Local Representation & Project Oversight
  • Asset & Property Registration Support
  • Relocation & Concierge Settlement
  • Dispute Resolution & Asset Recovery
  • Portfolio Asset Management

Policy & Legal Advisory

Navigating the frameworks that govern what’s possible.
