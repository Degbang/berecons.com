Our Story

We decided to
close the gap.

2020

Evidence.
Every decision.

We saw a persistent gap between the complexity of the challenges facing African organisations and the quality of advisory support available to them. Generic frameworks, imported methodologies, and a tendency to answer questions before understanding the context. We decided to close that gap.

Over five years, we have grown from a research and consulting practice into a fully integrated advisory firm, adding capabilities in systems thinking, digital solutions, programme management, and policy advisory as our clients’ challenges demanded them.

We are small by deliberate choice. Every engagement receives senior attention. Every recommendation is defensible. Every intervention is built for execution, not presentation.

Standards that guide every engagement.

We believe in four principles, applied as non-negotiable standards, not ideals. Each one is tested in every decision, from who gets assigned to a brief, to what is written, shared, and acted on.

01

Honesty

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what confirms the decision already made.

02

Rigour

We do not guess. We research, analyse, and verify before we advise. Every recommendation is defensible.

03

Context

African institutions operate in African realities. We understand both, and we build every recommendation from that foundation.

04

Integrity

A strategy disconnected from execution is a document. We connect research, design, technology, and delivery, and stand behind what we produce.
