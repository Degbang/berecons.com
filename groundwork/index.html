The problem we solve

The market is not broken. It is opaque.

Opacity is the environment in which most investment losses occur. Deals collapse not because the asset was not real, but because the verification was not done. Because no one asked the right questions before the money moved.

Where transactions fail

  • Double-allocation of the same parcel
  • Title fraud and forged documentation
  • Contractors who abandon projects mid-build
  • Informal agreements with no legal recourse
  • Developer overpricing with no benchmarking
  • No representation for remote investors
  • Regulatory non-compliance found too late

The Berecons approach

We do not remove risk. We document it, quantify it, and build the structural safeguards that ensure you are never exposed to risks you did not knowingly accept.

Independent verification. Process documentation. No conflicts of interest. Local presence, international standards.

How Groundwork works

Four steps to a protected transaction

Select each step to read the detail.

01

Consultation

A structured conversation by call, video, or in person. We listen to your objective, the asset or investment in question, and your timeline. No fee for the initial consultation and no obligation to proceed.

02

Scoping and proposal

Within 48 hours, we deliver a formal scope of objectives, a preliminary engagement framework, and a fixed-fee investment structure. You will know exactly what milestones will be achieved, the precise timeframe, and the exact cost. Nothing proceeds until terms are mutually agreed and confirmed in writing.

03

Execution

We carry out the agreed verification, due diligence, oversight, or registration work according to scope. Progress is documented. Findings are recorded. You receive structured updates throughout.

04

Delivery and advisory

You receive the final written output, transaction record, registration update, or oversight summary. Where findings raise concerns, we advise you before any irreversible step is taken.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Do I need to be in Ghana to use your services?

No. Many Groundwork engagements are built for remote clients who need verified local representation before capital moves.

Is Berecons LLC a law firm?

No. We provide strategic advisory, verification, and process management. Where formal legal representation is required, we work alongside qualified legal practitioners.

Why are fees not listed on the page?

Because every engagement is scoped individually. We prefer accurate fixed-fee proposals to broad public ranges that do not reflect the actual work required.

When do I pay?

Only after scope, milestones, and fee structure have been confirmed in writing. No engagement begins on assumptions.

Do you have relationships with developers or agents?

No financial interest in any property, developer, or contractor we introduce. Groundwork is designed to remain independent.

Can I bundle services?

Yes. Where a matter spans acquisition, registration, oversight, or settlement, we scope a combined engagement and explain the fee model before work starts.
