The Problem We Solve

The Ghana property market is not broken.
It is opaque.

Opacity is the environment in which most investment losses occur. Deals collapse not because the asset was not real, but because the verification was not done. Because the agreement was informal. Because the principal was not present. Because no one asked the right questions before the money moved.

These are the structural friction points Berecons LLC eliminates:

  • Double-allocation of land, the same parcel sold to multiple buyers
  • Title fraud and forged documentation from sellers or agents
  • Unverified contractors who collect deposits and abandon projects
  • Informal agreements that provide no legal recourse when disputes arise
  • Developer overpricing with no independent cost benchmarking
  • Extortionate facilitation fees for standard statutory processes
  • Absent representation, remote investors with no eyes on the ground
  • Regulatory non-compliance discovered after capital is committed

The Berecons Groundwork Response

We do not remove risk. We document it, quantify it, and build the safeguards.

Our approach is systems-driven. Every step is documented. Every finding is written. You hold the record, regardless of what happens on the ground.

  • Independent Verification We verify everything in writing. If it cannot be verified, we say so before you commit.
  • Process Documentation Every step of your transaction is documented. You hold the record.
  • No Conflicts of Interest We are retained by you, not by the vendor, developer, or agent.
  • Local Presence, Global Standards We operate in Accra. We apply international due diligence standards to that local context.

Land & Property Acquisition Security

No title, no transfer. No exceptions.

Acquiring land or property in Ghana without independent verification is not a calculated risk. It is an unquantified one. Berecons LLC conducts comprehensive pre-acquisition security checks that establish the verifiable status of any asset before our client commits capital. We work independently of the selling agent, the developer, and the vendor, so our findings reflect the asset’s actual condition, not the transaction’s commercial interest.

  • Independent title search at the Lands Commission and relevant District Offices
  • Parcel history verification, tracing ownership lineage and identifying prior claims
  • Boundary demarcation and physical survey coordination with licensed surveyors
  • Double-allocation screening, cross-referencing against known disputed parcels
  • Encumbrance and charge verification, mortgages, caveats, court orders affecting the title
  • Planning and zoning status confirmation with appropriate municipal authorities
  • Developer background and track record verification for off-plan acquisitions
  • Indenture and site plan review for legal sufficiency before signing
  • Stool land and family land identification, particularly for Customary Land Secretariat areas

What this prevents

Double-allocation losses; title fraud; purchasing encumbered assets; acquiring land in disputed or restricted zones; signing agreements on assets whose ownership cannot be legally established.

Asset & Property Registration Services

Registered. Recorded. Protected.

An unregistered asset in Ghana is a vulnerable one. The documentation process, from indenture preparation and survey plan coordination to Lands Commission submission and title registration, is navigable but exacting. Berecons LLC manages the full registration lifecycle for property and asset transactions, ensuring that our clients hold legally enforceable title in their name, correctly recorded, with no outstanding administrative exposure.

  • Indenture preparation and execution coordination with vendors and legal counsel
  • Site plan coordination with licensed surveyors and submission to the Lands Commission
  • Land title registration and document stamping at the Lands Commission
  • Stamp Duty assessment and payment management
  • Internal Revenue Authority (IRA) compliance for property transactions
  • Land Use and Spatial Planning Authority (LUSPA) documentation where required
  • Estate and inheritance registration, processing inherited land into the beneficiary’s name
  • Title conversion, converting customary documentation into Statutory Freehold or Leasehold title

What this prevents

Asset exposure through unregistered title; inheritance disputes from informally documented family land; double-allocation vulnerability; administrative penalties for non-compliance with Stamp Duty obligations.
