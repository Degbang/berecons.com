The problem we solve

The market is not broken.
It is opaque.

Opacity is the environment in which most investment losses occur. Deals collapse not because the asset was not real, but because the verification was not done. Because no one asked the right questions before the money moved.

Where transactions fail

  1. Double-allocation of the same parcel
  2. Title fraud and forged documentation
  3. Contractors who abandon projects mid-build
  4. Informal agreements with no legal recourse
  5. Developer overpricing with no benchmarking
  6. No representation for remote investors
  7. Regulatory non-compliance found too late
The Berecons approach

We do not remove risk. We document it, quantify it, and build the structural safeguards that ensure you are never exposed to risks you did not knowingly accept.

Independent verification. Process documentation. No conflicts of interest. Local presence, international standards.

Our capabilities

01

Land & Property Acquisition Security

No title, no transfer. No exceptions.

Comprehensive pre-acquisition checks that establish the verifiable status of any asset before capital is committed. We work independently of the selling agent and vendor, so our findings reflect the asset's actual condition, not the transaction's commercial interest.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

If your question is not here, contact us directly; we respond to every enquiry.

Do I need to be in Ghana to use your services?

No. Many Groundwork engagements are built for remote clients who need verified local representation before capital moves.

Is Berecons LLC a law firm?

No. We provide strategic advisory, verification, and process management. Where formal legal representation is required, we work alongside qualified legal practitioners.

Why are fees not listed on the page?

Because every engagement is scoped individually. We prefer accurate fixed-fee proposals to broad public ranges that do not reflect the actual work required.

When do I pay?

Only after scope, milestones, and fee structure have been confirmed in writing. No engagement begins on assumptions.

Do you have relationships with developers or agents?

No financial interest in any property, developer, or contractor we introduce. Groundwork is designed to remain independent at every stage.

Can I bundle services?

Yes. Where a matter spans acquisition, registration, oversight, or settlement, we scope a combined engagement and explain the fee model before work starts.
